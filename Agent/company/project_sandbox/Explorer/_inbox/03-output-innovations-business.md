## B. Own-business approaches, ranked for Sin (build lens)

Scored against the 13-parameter framework; top differentiators: (P2) vendor-substitutability, (P3) proprietary data, (P12) token-deflation direction, (P13) accountability anchor.

### Rank 1 — Thai/SEA RL environments, evals & verifiers (Axis 1)
- **What:** build RL environments + eval harnesses + verifiers for Thai/SEA verticals (insurance claims, Thai legal, banking compliance, logistics). Sell to labs (who reportedly budget $1B+/yr for environments; exclusives price 4-5x) and to enterprises validating agents.
- **Moat:** task corpus + reward logic + domain experts + regression history — doesn't travel with an API. Sin's insurance-domain access = "สิ่งที่เขาไม่มีทางมี".
- **P12:** token deflation HELPS (cheaper to generate/test). **P2:** labs are the customer, not the competitor. ✅ strongest fit.
- **First test:** one lab/enterprise pays for a pilot within 3 months of demo.

### Rank 2 — AI FinOps / inference unit-economics tooling (Axis 2)
- **What:** token+GPU granular cost telemetry, model routing, tokens-per-watt optimization. Validated gap: practitioners report no commercial tool does granular token+LLM+GPU monitoring at enterprise scale; 58% of orgs name AI cost mgmt their #1 skill gap.
- **Moat:** medium (P8 decay risk) — must convert into proprietary benchmark data over time. Direct extension of Sin's day job.
- **First test:** 3 paying design partners; savings-share pricing.

### Rank 3 — Services-as-software for regulated Thai back-office (Axis 3)
- **What:** outcome-priced AI services where a licensed/accountable entity must sign (insurance claims processing, PDPA compliance, audit prep). Capture labor budgets, not software budgets.
- **Moat:** P13 accountability + P6 regulatory; strengthens as AI improves (P12 favorable).
- **First test:** one Thai SME pays per-outcome within 60 days.

### Rank 4 — SEA data-center "missing middle" software (Axis 2×3)
- **What:** DCIM/PUE/BOI-compliance reporting + energy telemetry for 3-25MW operators riding Thailand's ~$16B DC buildout; grid-constraint intelligence (TH eastern grid reserve 5.2%).
- **Moat:** local regulatory + relationship; geography = the unfair asset.
- **First test:** one operator LOI during construction phase.

### Rank 5 — Agent observability / identity / governance (ties to Regent AI)
- **What:** agent audit trails, permissioning, eval-in-production. Governance adoption lags deployment 7-8x; EU AI Act agent guidance still missing = pre-regulation window (Axis 4).
- **Moat:** early standards position; risk = platform vendors absorb it (P2 medium).
- **First test:** paid pilot with an enterprise deploying agents in production.

### Anti-patterns (do NOT build)
- Pure API wrappers ("Claude for X") — fails P2/P12; vendor gets your raw material cheaper than you
- Decentralized data marketplaces — demand side proven absent (DePIN: ~$19B cap peak vs ~$72M real revenue)
- Capital-intensive hardware (chips, robots, reactors, DePIN devices) — violates Sin's tier rule
- Anything whose usage is subsidized/token-incentivized rather than paid

### Kill/scale thresholds (apply to every build)
- Scale: paying customer within 90 days of demo; >60% gross margin; NRR >100%
- Kill: 18 months below $1K MRR; usage only when subsidized; model vendor ships it as a feature and churn follows
