# 13 — Buyability Status & IPO Watchlist

> The missing dimension across files 07, 10, 11 and 12: **can you actually buy it?**
> Every group has names that are structurally important but not purchasable. This file tracks them so that when access opens, nothing is missed.

---

## Status taxonomy

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ | **Buyable now** — listed and accessible | Normal screening |
| 🟡 | **Buyable, restricted access** — foreign exchange, ADR-only, or broker-dependent | Check your broker first |
| 🔒 | **Private, plausible IPO** — venture-backed, scale achieved | **Watchlist. This is the group that matters most** |
| 🏛️ | **Foundation / family / state owned** — structurally unlikely to ever list | Find the proxy; stop waiting |
| ❌ | **Absorbed** — acquired, gone permanently | Delete from the list; use the acquirer |
| ⛔ | **Restricted** — sanctions, capital controls, or policy risk | Note for competitive tracking, not for buying |

**The rule that emerged from the ASML work:** the deeper upstream you go, the higher the 🏛️ ratio. **The tightest monopolies in the AI stack are the least investable** — that is a structural feature of this industry, not an accident.

# HOW THIS CHANGES THE WORKFLOW

Add buyability as **step 0** of screening, before anything else:

```
0. Buyable?  ✅ → screen normally
             🟡 → check broker access first
             🔒 → move to IPO watchlist, set an alert, stop researching valuation
             🏛️ → find the proxy, remove from the active list
             ❌ → delete, replace with the acquirer
             ⛔ → track for competitive intelligence only
1. Revenue actually AI-exposed?
2. Component supplier or owner of a constrained asset?
3. Customer concentration?
4. Price already discounting 2030?
5. Which era does it survive?
```

**Two structural observations worth carrying:**

**① The 🏛️ concentration rises with upstream depth.** Zeiss, TRUMPF, Schott, Heraeus, VDL, EV Group, Sibelco — the deepest chokepoints are foundation- or family-owned and will not list. For those, **ASML is the aggregation point**, which partly explains its valuation: you are paying for access to a supply chain you cannot otherwise touch.

**② The 🔒 list is where the next decade's listings come from.** Databricks, Cerebras, Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceX, Stripe, Anduril, Figure, Fervo, Form Energy, PsiQuantum. **Track them now so you're not learning the story on IPO day** — that is the reverse of the "18 months late" pattern.

**Suggested cadence:** review the 🔒 table quarterly for S-1 filings and funding-round news. That is roughly 30 minutes per quarter and is the cheapest edge in this entire map.
