## How to read this

Three structural findings from research shape the whole map:

**① Copper is the real story, not chips.** Copper accounts for **82-83% of total projected mineral demand from AI data centers**, with grid infrastructure consuming nearly two-thirds of that. Grain-oriented electrical steel and rare-earth permanent magnets are named as critical but overlooked bottlenecks. And the binding constraint is **processing and refining capacity, not geological scarcity** — which points investment toward processing, metallurgy and grid infrastructure rather than toward miners.

**② The second tier is where leadership is moving.** Leadership has extended beyond first-order beneficiaries, with the strongest opportunities emerging through discrete expressions within the infrastructure, power and industrial layers. Outcomes depend less on sector or geography than on the specific role a company plays in the investment cycle.

**③ The adopters are the underexplored half.** Equity value in major technology waves accrues not only to technology suppliers but to the companies that apply the technology most effectively. The entire corporate economy is now trying to figure out how to use AI; the next stage comes from identifying who converts the infrastructure layer into better business economics.

**Scale anchor:** Microsoft, Google, Amazon and Meta together committed **>$700B in 2026 capital spending**, guiding higher for 2027.

### C1. Copper 🔑 ⚠️
**The single largest material exposure.** Data center capacity is estimated to require ~500kt additional copper by 2027, with Chinese data center buildout potentially pushing that substantially higher. Structural undersupply; ~60% of refined copper comes from three countries.
- [ ] FCX Freeport-McMoRan · SCCO Southern Copper · TECK Teck · GLEN.L Glencore · ANTO.L Antofagasta

# PART H — WHAT TO WATCH SKEPTICALLY

|---|---|
| Pure-play SMR developers | IEA projects only 10-25 GWe globally by 2035; prices discount far more |

| Companies selling only to 3-4 hyperscalers | Concentration is not a moat |

# SCREENING QUESTIONS

Applied per name, in this order:

1. **Still listed and independent?** File 07 found 9 of 100 failed here.
2. **What share of revenue is actually AI/data-centre exposed?** Many industrials are <10%.
3. **Component supplier, or owner of a constrained asset?** Constrained assets — grid interconnection, licensed fuel, land, long-lead manufacturing, processing capacity — survive an overbuild. Components compete away.
4. **Who is the customer?** Four hyperscalers is concentration risk.
5. **Is the AI angle in the revenue or only the press release?**
6. **Does the price already discount 2030?**
7. **Which era does this survive?** Buildout (now) · operation (2028+) · consolidation (post-shakeout).
