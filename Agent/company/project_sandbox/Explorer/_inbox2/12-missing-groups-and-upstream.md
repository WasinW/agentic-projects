# PART 0 — VERIFYING THE ASML UPSTREAM NAMES

Your research is correct on the technology. **But all three names are unbuyable, and that is the important finding.**

| Name | Status | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| **Carl Zeiss** | ❌ **Private.** Owned by the Carl-Zeiss-Stiftung foundation. **Carl Zeiss Meditec (AFX.DE) is the only listed subsidiary — and it is medical technology, not semiconductor.** Zeiss SMT is unreachable via public markets | ✅ |
| **TRUMPF** | ❌ **Private.** Family-owned — Leibinger family 90%, Berthold Leibinger Stiftung 10%. €5.36bn revenue, 18,352 employees. No listing | ✅ |
| **Cymer** | ❌ **Acquired by ASML in 2013**, specifically to control the light source. Not separately investable | ✅ |

**The only public proxy for Zeiss SMT is ASML itself** — ASML paid €1bn for a 25% stake in Zeiss SMT in 2016, with the money funding Zeiss optics R&D for next-generation EUV.

**Why this matters structurally:** only ~15% of an EUV machine's components are built in-house; the remaining 85% come from other firms. So the deepest chokepoints in the entire AI stack are held by **private German Mittelstand companies that cannot be bought.** That is a genuine structural fact about this industry — the tightest monopolies are the least investable.

**And on competition, your document is right:** in EUV, ASML holds effectively 100% share with no competitor; in DUV it holds ~80-85%, with Nikon and Canon still present. China's SMEE and related efforts have produced basic domestic DUV but remain years behind on performance and scale.
