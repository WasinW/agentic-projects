# HOW TO USE THIS

**This is ~1,100 names across 67 groups.** No one screens that. Suggested reduction path:

1. **Kill by geography** — if you won't trade Japan/Korea/Taiwan/Europe, that removes ~40%
2. **Kill by market cap floor** — set a minimum; removes most ⚠️ names
3. **Kill by liquidity** — average daily volume floor
4. **Then apply the structural filters** — constrained asset vs component · revenue share actually AI-exposed · customer concentration · which era it survives
5. **Only then research individual names**

C1 copper · C2 rare earths · C3 GOES · B10 transmission · B7 battery storage · A14 neoclouds · G1/G3 Thailand
